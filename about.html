About Tat:vm

Founded by seasoned real estate professionals, Tatvm Corp Private Limited is being built on a solid foundation of experience, vision and a deep passion for creating homes that are unique.

Tat:vm hopes to bring a fresh perspective to Mumbai's urban landscape, because we believe that in a city where we spend more time in office and in traffic, a home should be a place ‘rooted in you’.

Our Guiding Principles

Tat:vm is based on five essential tattvas (fundamental elements) which are our guiding principles for every project.

We are laying down the blueprint to rise above the clutter by optimising space, natural sunlight, ventilation and need-based amenities – building not just homes that matter, but communities that thrive and legacies that stand the test of time.

Future-Ready Developments

We hope to ensure a more inclusive and sustainable development, by effectively addressing the key urban challenges of redevelopment and rehabilitation.

We will drive innovation with branded residences that carry the prestige and assurance of the brand, fulfil the aspirations of the larger society, create a stronger sense of belonging, and go on to build a formidable presence in the market.
