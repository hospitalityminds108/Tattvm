Tatvm Group – Shaping the Future of
Urban Living in Mumbai

At Tatvm Group, we are redefining the contours of urban development through a refined blend of architectural excellence, sustainable innovation, and uncompromising quality. Our expertise spans across residential, commercial, and redevelopment ventures, each meticulously crafted to deliver enduring value for stakeholders and communities alike.

Recognized as one of Mumbai's most dynamic and emerging real estate builders, our philosophy is rooted in the synergy of the five elemental pillars: space, light, air, vastu, and sustainability. We are proud to be transforming Mumbai's skyline with branded developments that embody innovation, integrity, and purposeful design—creating future-ready spaces that seamlessly integrate form and function.

Trusted Real Estate Developers in Mumbai

Founded by Rahul Maroo, seasoned real estate professional, Tatvm is being built on a solid foundation of experience, vision and a deep passion for creating homes that go beyond brick and mortar—they resonate with meaning, purpose, and long-term value. Our legacy is built on reliability, transparency, and a deep-rooted commitment to holistic urban growth.

We don't just build homes. We create landmarks that generations will admire, aspire to, and fall in love with. Every project we take on is designed to be the most iconic address in its market. A place that sets the standard, commands a premium, and becomes a point of pride for its residents. Whether crafting premium residential enclaves, modern commercial complexes, or mixed-use developments, Tatvm Group exemplifies strategic foresight and executional excellence.
