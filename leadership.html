Leadership

At Tatvm Corp, we believe strong leadership is the cornerstone of success. Led by the visionary zeal of Mr. Rahul Maroo, the company banks on his extensive real estate experience; having worked with India’s top developers, leading multifaceted projects, managing large-scale developments and driving visible transformations across Mumbai

The team at Tat:vm shares his commitment, working together to build a legacy that reflects the values of harmony, innovation and purpose.

Meet the helmsman at Tat:vm, who steers a team driven by a shared vision to transform lives and skylines.

Rahul Maroo

Founder & Managing Director

Tatvm Corp Private Limited

A first generation real estate developer who comes with 18+ years of experience in the industry, Mr. Rahul Maroo leads the company with a vision to pioneer a brand that is poised to set new standards for urban living - creating spaces that are 'rooted in you'.


Having contributed significantly to the city's real estate through luxury housing, redevelopment projects and slum rehabilitation over the last two decades, Mr. Maroo brings his expertise, passion and relentless commitment to changing the face of the city, for the larger good.
